The Mutineer Economy

Three core mechanisms engineered to align incentives and reward long-term crew members.

🏦

Dynamic Storm Taxes

Every sell of the native utility token $BOOTY is taxed dynamically between 2% to 8% based on market volatility (rough storm seas). 100% of these taxes feed the Plunder Vault.

🗳️

The Saturday Mutiny

Every Saturday, the Plunder Vault opens. NFT holders vote off-chain on how to distribute the loot. You choose: distribute 60% directly to the crew (Plunder) or use it to buyback & burn $BOOTY (Bury).

🪵

Walk the Plank

Holding your NFTs starts your Cabin Crew Streak. Your reward share and voting weight increases by +10% weekly up to a maximum of 2.0x (at 10 weeks). If you transfer or sell ANY mutineer, you walk the plank—your streak resets to zero.

Economic Flywheel

Unlike standard inflationary rewards tokens, $BOOTY operates on a volatility-driven loop:

  • 1. Trading Activity: High trading volatility increases the Dynamic Storm Tax.
  • 2. Plunder Accumulation: Taxes accumulate as $BOOTY tokens inside the Plunder Vault.
  • 3. Saturday Votes: If the crew votes to "Bury", tokens are burned, permanently reducing total supply. If they vote to "Plunder", tokens are re-distributed, incentivizing longer streaks.
